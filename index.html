Jing Wei (Nick) Chin

Researcher · Entrepreneur · Lecturer

I am a health AI researcher and technology entrepreneur working at the intersection of computer vision, machine learning, and non-invasive physiological monitoring — building contactless, medical-grade vital-sign sensing from ordinary cameras. I am Co-Founder & CTO of PanopticAI, a Hong Kong startup developing camera-based vital-sign monitoring (software as a medical device). I have also lectured part-time at HKUST.

My work spans deep-learning infrared thermography for fever screening, remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) for heart rate, heart-rate variability, blood oxygen, and blood pressure estimation, and the clinical validation of camera-based monitoring in emergency departments. See my publications for details.
